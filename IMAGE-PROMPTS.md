# Pflugerville Water Softener: Image Generation Prompts

Paste each prompt into your image generator (Midjourney, Flux, Ideogram, DALL-E, etc.).
Every prompt below already includes the shared style block, so each one works on its own.

## Specs for every image

- Aspect ratio: **16:9** (generate at 1408 x 768 or larger, then export to that size)
- Format: **WebP**, quality about 80, under 200 KB each
- Save into `src/assets/images/` using the exact filename shown above each prompt
- No text, logos, watermarks, signage, or readable labels anywhere in the image
- No people's faces (hands, backs, or silhouettes only)
- Keep the main subject in the **right two thirds** of the frame: on inner pages the image sits in the right column of the page hero and the left edge is often cropped on mobile

## Shared style block (already pasted into each prompt)

> Photorealistic editorial photograph, natural window light with soft warm highlights, shallow depth of field, clean modern suburban Central Texas home, color palette of deep teal (#0F6E78) and warm orange (#E65100) accents with neutral whites and stone tones, crisp and uncluttered, premium local-service-business look, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no faces, 16:9, high detail

## Local look to keep consistent

Pflugerville is a Texas suburb north-east of Austin. Homes are mostly 2000s to 2020s builds in light brick and Hill Country limestone with stucco, slab foundations, and attached garages. Softeners are normally installed in the garage or utility room, on the wall where the main water line enters the house. Landscaping is live oak, crape myrtle, and St. Augustine grass. Skies are bright Texas blue.

## After generating

1. Convert to WebP (for example with squoosh.app or `cwebp -q 80`) and rename to the exact filenames above.
2. Drop them into `src/assets/images/`.
3. Tell Claude: "I have saved the images, wire them in." It will import each one and pass it to `PageHero` as the `image` prop, and replace the homepage placeholders.
4. Add meaningful `alt` text per image while wiring (describes the scene and includes Pflugerville where natural).
